The Problem We Solve

Three Problems.
One Vision.

01

Vaccine Cold Chain

Vaccines must stay between 2 °C and 8 °C from factory to patient. In clinics without reliable power, the chain breaks at the last mile and doses are lost before they reach the people who need them most.

02

Industrial Energy

Heavy industry depends on an unreliable, carbon-intensive grid, while large amounts of high-grade heat leave its stacks every day unused, under a sun that is among the strongest in the world.

03

Communications Barrier

Twisted-light (OAM) beams can carry many channels at once, but atmospheric turbulence scrambles them within about a kilometre. We call it the Wits Bottleneck, and it has kept the idea in the laboratory.
