Solar-thermal cold chain

Vaccine cold storage powered by the sun

The Thermoka Cooler is an off-grid vaccine refrigerator that runs on stored solar heat, built for rural clinics across the Global South.

The problem

The last mile is where the cold chain breaks

Vaccines must stay between 2 °C and 8 °C from factory to patient. In clinics without reliable power, that chain fails and doses are lost.

The technology

Heat in. Cold out.

A lithium-bromide absorption cycle turns stored solar heat into cooling, with no compressor, built to run without the grid.
