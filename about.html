About us

A South African green-technology company, built in the Northern Cape.

Thermoka Green Energy (Pty) Ltd develops clean-energy technology for the places the power grid doesn't reliably reach.

Our mission

Ending cold-chain failure at the last mile.

We exist to fight cold-chain failure in off-grid and rural clinics across the Global South — in South Africa and beyond.

Our flagship project, the Thermoka Solar-Thermal Absorption Clinic Cooler, turns sunlight into dependable vaccine refrigeration without relying on the grid. It is the first of several clean-energy technologies Thermoka Green Energy is developing.

What guides us

How we build.

Built for the last mile

We design for the conditions rural clinics actually face — unreliable power, heat, and long distances from support.

Clean by design

Our technology runs on heat from the sun, not diesel generators or an unstable grid.

Engineered to standard

We work toward recognised standards like WHO PQS, so health programmes can trust what we deliver.

Beyond the cooler

Thermoka is our flagship — not our only project.

Thermoka Green Energy is developing further clean-energy and resource technologies for underserved communities. We'll share them here as they mature.

We're also building our engineering team. If you're a mechanical, thermal or embedded-systems engineer who wants to help build and test the Thermoka prototype, we'd like to hear from you.
